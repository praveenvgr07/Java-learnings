Stream :
    => A stream is a sequence of elements used to process the data efficiently using operation like 
    filtering , sorting , and mapping


Types of Stream:

    1)Binary Stream(Byte).=> it handles the 0's and 1's(png,jpg).
    2)Character Stream.=> it handles the text , char , word.

Types of Binary Stream:

    1)Input Stream. => Read
    2)Output Stream. => write

Types of Character :

    1)Reader.
    2)Writer.

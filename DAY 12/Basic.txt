how it store the values:
HashSet:
formula:
    =>adding element % size;
    Example:
        s.add(10);
        s.add(25);
        s.add(30);
        s.add(40);
        s.add(50);
        10%5 = 0 it will store in the 0th  index.
        =>it store in unorder and it is fast.(linkedhashset is stored in order and it is slow)
TreeSet is stored in the sorted manner.      
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
            |       HashSet        |       Linkedhashset              | TreeSet         | 
------------|----------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
duplicate   |      no              |            no                    |   no            |
order       |      unorder         |            yes                   |   sorted+order  |
fast/slow   |      fast            |            fast compare to tree  |   slow          |
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
